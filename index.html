That is one capability among several, and it comes from the same habit: deciding what a product actually needs before choosing what to build it with. AI is part of what I ship and part of how I work. I integrate language models behind a provider-agnostic layer so a product isn't tied to one vendor, and I designed and delivered a two-day workshop teaching others to use these tools to build and release real software — with the emphasis on judgement rather than tooling. Almost everything I build ships in Arabic, English and Turkish with full right-to-left support. I'm the founder of BinAref, where I handle the products, the systems behind them and the brand around them.
